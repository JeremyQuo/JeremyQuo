## Selected Publications

### 1. Comprehensive comparison of the third-generation sequencing tools for bacterial 6mA profiling

**Nature Communications**, 2025, 16, 3982  
**Co-first author**

This study provides a systematic comparison of sequencing and computational tools for bacterial DNA N6-methyladenine (6mA) profiling.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-59187-2)

---

### 2. Enhanced detection of RNA modifications in *Escherichia coli* utilizing direct RNA sequencing

**Cell Reports Methods**, 2025, 5, 101168  
**First author**

We developed **nanoSundial**, a comparative method for detecting bacterial RNA modifications using nanopore direct RNA sequencing and native/IVT RNA controls.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crmeth.2025.101168) ·
[View the code](https://github.com/lrslab/nanoSundial)

---

### 3. Analysis of bacterial transcriptome and epitranscriptome using nanopore direct RNA sequencing

**Nucleic Acids Research**, 2024, 52(15), 8746–8762

This study established a nanopore direct RNA sequencing workflow for bacterial transcriptome and epitranscriptome analysis.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkae601) ·
[View the analysis scripts](https://github.com/JeremyQuo/ONT_DRS_bacteria_script)
